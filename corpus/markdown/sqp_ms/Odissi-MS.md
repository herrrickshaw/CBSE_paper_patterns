14.   Jayadev was a renowned Sanskrit poet who lived during the 12 th century. 6
      He is best known for his epic poem Geet Govinda, which focuses on the
      divine love of lord Krishna and cowherdess Radha that transcends the
      physical aspects in order to reach spiritual unity. Geet Govinda is an
      immortal work of literature that has nurtured India with its rich devotional
      compositions over centuries.
      Since the stanzas of Geet Govind are usually eight in number, the songs
      are also referred to as Ashtapadi. The Ashtapadis are unique compositions
      in which the poet Jayadev has specified the taal and raaga for each
      Ashtapadi. The Ashtapadis of Geet Govind are generally performed as
      abhinaya compositions in all classical dances of India.
