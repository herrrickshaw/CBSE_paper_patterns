before his death. His two sons, Bhim (aged 10) and Arjun (aged 11) have
to drop out of school and work in a glass factory to make their ends meet.

common for most of the families in village Bhandha from where he was
reporting. He also wrote a letter to the Hon'ble Supreme Court requesting

Is Pramod justified in refusing to carry on as a defense lawyer for
