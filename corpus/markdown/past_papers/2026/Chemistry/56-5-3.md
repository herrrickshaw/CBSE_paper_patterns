       type of electrode used. Oxidising and reducing species present in the
       electrolytic cell and their standard electrode potential too, affect the

       people’s daily lives, whether it is for the manufacturing of aluminium,

       laws of electrolysis. Faraday’s laws of electrolysis provide a basis for
