     Khawvel pum thlirin thil kan chin/thar chhuah a ngai. Kuthnathawktute
     din chhuah theihnan Sawrkar pawhin tih tur a nei. Mithiam hna hmu lo an
     awm nghek nghuk loh nan man power planning, educational planning
     sawrkarin a ngaih pawimawh a ngai. Heng zawng zawng hi a kal fuh
     hunah tuna zahthlaka kan ngaih, kut hnathawh ngei hi a manhla zia leh
