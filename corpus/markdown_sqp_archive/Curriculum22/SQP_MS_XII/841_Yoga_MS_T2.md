           Clinical course: Signs and symptoms of Asthma
           Asthma is characterized by episodic dyspnea (difficulty in breathing), wheezing and
           cough.
           *Difficulty in expiration.
           * Tightness of the chest/discomfort in the chest may be seen.
           * Attacks last from one to several hours.
           * Severe attacks may affect the heart and circulatory system.
           *Severe attack, not responsive to usual therapy is called “status asthmaticus” and is a
           medical emergency.
           * Hypercarbia (increased level of CO2 ), acidosis and hypoxia (decreased O2 level) is rare
           in Asthma.
