36 The principles that guide the indexers in the choice and rendering of subject                (3)
    headings in SLSH are ‘Specific Entry’, ‘Common Usage’ and ‘Uniformity’.
        a) Specific Entry: A work should be entered under the most specific subject
       heading which accurately and precisely represents the content of the book. If a
                                                                                            1
       reader wants a book about bridges, the direct approach is to consult the catalogue
       under the heading Bridges, not under the large topic Engineering, or even the
       more restricted field, Civil engineering.
        b) Common Usage: The subject heading chosen to express the contents of the
                                                                                            1

        c) Uniformity: One uniform heading must be selected from several synonyms,

     guidelines.                                                                                            1

                                                                                                        1

                                                                                                        1
