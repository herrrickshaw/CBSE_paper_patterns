            the total tour costing. Technical cost includes the acquisition of new software,

            and ferry tickets.
        5) Training and Development: The cost of training staff is indirectly included in
            total cost. Tour operators hire the trainers for in-house training or send the
            staff and senior managers to professional institutes.

            promotion. The sales promotion includes discounts, commission and other

            package tour.
        8) Payment of Interest: Tour operators incur the cost of payment of interest to

        9) Depreciation of Assets: Tour operators incur depreciation cost in the long run

        10) Miscellaneous Cost: This cost includes porterage charges at airports and
            railway stations, tips at hotels or airports, entrance fees, insurance premiums,
