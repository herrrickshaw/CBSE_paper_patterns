           following:

               cold discharge.

Q. 20   Identify different types of combs used for       CBSE   3   93   4
        the hairstyle.

        Ans: Combs are used to style hairs in
        different manner. Combs are classified on
        the basis of their different shapes and sizes
        are as follows:

        1: Flat back brush: It is used while root
        lifting if the hair is short.

        2: Tail or teaser comb: It is used for
        sectioning the hair. The teeth of this comb
        are tightly spaced and the comb has a long.

        3: Radial brush: It is most suitable for
        inducing curls, root lift and volume in all
        hair lengths.

        4: Vent brush: It is a light weight brush with
        small spaces that allow the heat from the
        dryer to flow into the hair.

        5: Dressing comb: Its teeth are medium
        spaced and it is used for styling and
        finishing blow dry.

        6: Wide toothed or rake comb: The teeth of
        such a comb are widely spaced and used for
        untangling wet or dry hair.
