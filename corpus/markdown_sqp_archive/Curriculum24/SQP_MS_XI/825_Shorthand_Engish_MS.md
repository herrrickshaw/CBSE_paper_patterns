Q. 20 When the two strokes are joined together          CBSE Study Material     Unit 2   Pg. no.    4
      without lifting the pen or pencil it is called                                     15-16
      joining of strokes.
      Rules for joining are as follows:
      1. pen or pencil should not be lifted in
      between while joining the consonants.
      2. The 2nd stroke should commence at the end
      of 1st stroke and 3rd should commence at the
      end of 2nd and so on.
      3. While joining the strokes must be written in
      the same direction as when standing alone.
      4. While joining the length, angle, impression
