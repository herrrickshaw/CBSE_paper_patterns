Q. 17 Discharging action: When a battery           CBSE   1    58      4
      is being discharged,current flow from
      the negative pole to the positive pole.
      The hydrogen ions of electrolyte
      (diluted sulphuric acid) move to the
      positive plate and combine with
      oxygen to form water. The sulphate
      ions combine with lead to form lead
      sulphate. When the battery is fully
      discharged the specific gravity of
      electrolyte reaches to 1.160.
      Charging action: When a battery is
      being charged, the current flows from
      the positive pole to the negative pole.
      The oxygen ions move to the positive
      plate where they displace the
      sulphate ions simultaneously move
      back from the negative plates into the
      electrolyte. This turns the negative
      plates back into lead.
