Q. 23   The Two-Step Flow Theory, proposed          CBSE Study Material   Unit 3   4
        by Paul Lazarsfeld, suggests that
        media messages do not directly
        influence the public. Instead, they first
        reach opinion leaders—people who
        are more engaged with media and
        well-informed. These leaders then
        interpret the information and pass it
        on to others in their social circle.
        This theory challenges the idea that
        media affects everyone equally. It
        shows that personal influence plays a
        big role in shaping opinions.
        Example 1: A student follows a
        popular YouTuber (opinion leader)
        who discusses climate change. The
        student then shares this information
        with friends, influencing their views.
        Example 2: During elections, people
        may form opinions based on what a
        trusted family member or community
        leader says about a candidate, rather
        than on direct exposure to news.
