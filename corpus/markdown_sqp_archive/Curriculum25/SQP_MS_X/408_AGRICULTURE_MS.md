Fire Blight (Erwinia amylovora):
This disease is caused by bacteria. The symptoms are seen as
distinct fire-like appearance on infected plant parts. The shoot
tips wilt and droop without browning. Secretion of golden
colored bacterial ooze is seen on the stem. In fruits, Necrotic
spots andoozing lesions are observed.
Control: The affected trees and host plants should be collected
and burnt immediately. Sprays of streptomycin can control the
infection in spring blossom of apple.
Powdery Mildew (Podosphaera leucotricha): The diseases are

coating in patches on both sides of the leaves, and young
shoots. The affected leaves turn pale and curl up. Affected
shoots remain weak and
immature. In case of severe infection, leaf fall and pre-mature
fruit drop may occur.
