        Uses of Specular map:

        The uses of Specular Maps are:

        1. Some surfaces can be made
        shinier than others by mapping a
        texture to the specular attribute
        of an object’s material.

        2. Shiny objects reflect light
        directly but matte objects diffuse
        light.

        3. Specular highlights show the
        places on the object where the
        light sources are reflected.

        4. Specular highlights depend
        directly on the view (camera), not
        on the position of the light, like
        diffuse shading does.
        5. The size of a specular highlight
        on a surface makes the surface
        look either flat or shiny.
