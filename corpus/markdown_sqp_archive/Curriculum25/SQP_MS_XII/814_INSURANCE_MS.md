Q. 18 A fire policy containing an average      CBSE Study    Unit 2   Page no.50    3
      clause is called Average Policy. An       Material
      average policy requires the insurer to
      pay that portion of actual loss as the
      Insurance bears to the actual value of
      the property at the time of loss.
      Example: If the actual value of the
      property is Rs. 10,00,000 and the
      same is insured for Rs. 8,00,000 and
      loss on account of fire is Rs. 2,00,000.
      In such case the Insured will get
      8,00,000/10,00,000*2,00,000=
      1,60,000
      The balance of Rs.40,000 shall be
      borne by the Insured himself.
      However, if the insured amount is
      equal to the value of the property or
      more than that he will get
      compensation of the entire loss i.e.
      2,00,000.
