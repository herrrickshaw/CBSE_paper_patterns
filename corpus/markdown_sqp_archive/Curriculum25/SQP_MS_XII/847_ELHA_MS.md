        service management (ITSM) that aligns IT services with business needs. Key
        requirements of ITIL for businesses include:
        1. Service Strategy: Develop a clear IT service strategy aligned with business goals.
            This involves understanding customer needs, market spaces, and formulating
            policies to manage services.
        2. Service Design: Create IT services and processes that meet business
            requirements. This includes designing services, service management systems,
            and service metrics.

            change management, release and deployment management, and service testing
            to mitigate risks.

            management to ensure stability and service quality.

            processes to ensure they remain aligned with business objectives. This involves
            analyzing performance data and implementing enhancements.
