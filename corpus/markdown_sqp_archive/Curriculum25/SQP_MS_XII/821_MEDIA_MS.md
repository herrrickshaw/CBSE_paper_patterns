Main stages included in Pre-
Production are-
a) Storyboarding - Storyboarding is
an essential stage of the animation
process that helps to finalize the
development of the storyline.
b) Character design- Character design
involves developing the appearance
and features of characters in an
animation
c) Layout- Layouts are done once the
storyboards have been approved and
involve designing locations and
costumes. The layout department
works closely with the director to
stage the scenes and show the
various characters’ positions
throughout the course of each shot.
d) Model Sheets – Model sheets are
precisely drawn groups of pictures
that show all the possible
expressions and poses of a character
e) Animatic- An animatic is a timed
moving version of the storyboard
made up of individual frames taken
from the storyboard and is often
accompanied by audio.
