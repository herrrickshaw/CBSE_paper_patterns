or English                                    7X 2 =14 Marks

            (a) Hakim Omar Khayyam Neyshaburi is one of the great scholars
            of Iran. He was a mathematician, an astrologer and poet of his time.
            His ancestors were from Neyshabur. He was born in 5th century in
            the city of Neyshabur and he died in the same city in 517. Now the
            tomb of 'Umar Khayyam is near the city of Neyshabur.

            (b) A cat reached a fox. The cat, who thought the fox was a clever
            animal, greeted him and said, "How are you doing?" The proud fox
            looked at the cat and said, "Poor! Rat hunter! How dare you ask me?
            How much information do you even have? How many arts do you
            have? The cat embarrassedly said I only have one art. The fox asked,
            "What art?" and the cat said, "When the dogs follow me, I can jump
            on the tree and save my life." The fox laughed and said, just only
            this? However, I have 100 arts. I feel sorry for you, and I want to
            teach you how to deal with dogs.

            (c) Malik Jamshid said, "Oh damsel, I will take you out of here.
            Afterwards, he picked up Parizad on his compound and drove off.
            When he reached the gate of the city, he remembered that he had
            forgotten the flowers. Parizad said, "I am the daughter of the fairy
            king. When I laugh, it sheds flowers from my laughter, and if I cry,
            it sheds pearl from my tears. Malik Jamshid was delighted and
            thankful to God for excellence. Then he brought Parizad home,
            asked for a judge, and he married with her.
