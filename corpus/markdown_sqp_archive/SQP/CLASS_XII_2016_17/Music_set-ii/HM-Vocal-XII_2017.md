                                 Sample Question Paper 2017
                                             Class – XII
Time allowed: 03 hours                                                             Maximum Marks: 30

Instructions:

       Attempt any five questions.
       All questions carry equal marks.


   1. Define any form of the following -

        Murchhana, Alankar, Gram, Varna, Gamak, Tana


   2. Give the detailed description of any two of the following Ragas -

        (i)     Bhairav
        (ii)    Bageshri
        (iii)   Bhimpalasi



   3. Write the notation of a Vilambit Khayal in one of the prescribed Raga

                                                       OR

   5. Discuss the development of ‘classification of Ragas’ in Indian Music.

                                                       OR

        Throw light on ‘Time Theory of Ragas’.
