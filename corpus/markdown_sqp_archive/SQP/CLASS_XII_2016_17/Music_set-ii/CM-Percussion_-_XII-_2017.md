                       Carnatic Music (Percussion Instrumental)
                                    Code no.:033 (Theory)
                                Sample Question Paper 2017
                                            Class – XII
Time allowed: 03 hours                                                           Maximum Marks: 30

Instructions:

   1) Answer any five of the following questions.
   2) Question nos. 1 & 2 are compulsory.
   3) All questions carry equal marks.


   1. Notate a short Mohra and Korvai for a Misrachapu or Khanda chapu tala.


   2. Evaluate he contribution of chaturdandi Prakasika with special reference to tala.



   3. Explain the life sketch and contribution of Palani Subramanya Pillai to the art of Mridangam.


   4. Describe the following:
      (a) Shadangas
      (b) Trikalam
      (c) Tiruppugazh Talas
      (d) Teka

   5. Classify percussion instruments and describe any two upapauua vadhya’s construction and
      techniques.

   6. Explain the role of Tani avartanam in a Carnatic Music concert.


   7. Write short notes on any three of the following:
      (a) Kaalapramaanam
      (b) Ghumki
      (c) Choru-Vaaru
      (d) Gettu Vadhyam
