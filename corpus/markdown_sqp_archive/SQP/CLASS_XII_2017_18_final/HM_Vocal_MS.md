## page 1

```
                                HINDUSTANI MUSIC
                                 Vocal (Code No.034)
                                     Class – XII
                                Answer Key (2017-18)


1. Definition and varieties of the musical terms.

2. ‘Time Theory of Ragas’ based on the following:
   i.      Poorvang – Uttarang
   ii.     Madhyam Swara
   iii.    Day-Night
   iv.     Nature of Swaras
                                           Or
   ‘Classification of Ragas’ based on the following:
   i.      Raga Ragini System
   ii.     Dasavidh Raga System
   iii.    Ragang System
   iv.     Thaat Raga System

3. i. Total number of chapters in the text.
   ii. Musical contents in each chapter.
   iii. Special emphasis on establishment of swaras on Veena’s strings.


4. Thaat, Vadi, Samvadi, Jati, Time, Nature of Swaras, Varjit Swaras, Aroha, Avaroha,
   Pakad, Swara Vistar and Special Features.


5. i. Bhimpalasi
   ii. Bageshri


6. Notes and words of Tarana (Sthai and Antara) with Matra, Vibhag and Tala Signs in any
   one of the prescribed Raga.
                                           OR

   Notes and words of a Drut Khayal ( Sthai and Antara) with Matra, Vibhag and Tala signs
   in a prescribed Night Raga.
```
