                              Sample Marking Scheme
                   Senior School Certificate Examination 2018 - XII
                    GRAPHICS (THEORY) (History of India Art)

All the instructions mentioned in the question paper must be adhered by all the candidates.

    1. This is a knowledge based and long answer type question. A candidate is expected to cover
       following points in the answer:
       The Bengal School of Painting was the outcome of the Swadeshi Movement. With the coming of
       the British Empire in India, there was a decay of the Mughal Rajasthani and Pahari Schools
       towards the 19th century. Mr. E.B. Havell encouraged Traditional Indian Art and Indian Artist
       especially Abanindranath Tagore, the pioneer of the movement along with his worthy pupils who
       tried to regenerate a National Style of Painting.
       The exponents of the Bengal School changed with the newly awakened sense of Nationalism,
       derived their themes from the Indian epics, mythology, classical literature, romantic past and even
       from the contemporary National Freedom Movement, which succeeded in re-generality the wave
       of patriotism, re-establishing Indian cultural values, restoring the pride of the Indians and also in
       awakening the social and political consciousness, so this is also called the ‘Renaissance’ in the
       contemporary Indian art. It is also called the ‘Revivalistic Movement’.
       Abanindranath Tagore and his disciples painted the national leaders and Swarajya Movement.
       Nanadlal Bose and the disciples decorated the congress Pandals and the congress session villages
       such as at Haripura and Faizpur with posters (panels) reflecting the life of a common Indian
       people Asit Kumar Haldhar, Kshitindranath Majumdar and Mohd. Abdul R Chughtai etc through
       their paintnigs gave a tremendous moral boost to the Indianalistic Movement. D. P.
       Roychowdhary also draws attention of the general public not only by painting the national leaders
       but also providing many remarkable sculptures of national leaders and Swarajya Movement.
                                                                                                    5 marks

    2. This is a question of understanding and long answer type. A candidate is expected to appreciate
       any of the following contemporary (modern) Indian graphic-prints included in the course of study
       duly based on the given parameters as mentioned in the question:                        5 marks
       (a) Children
       (b) Of walls
       (c) Whirlpool

        Division of marks:
        (a) name of the graphic-artist                    1 mark
        (b) medium and technique                          1 mark
        (c) subject-matter                                1½ marks
        (d) composition                                   1½ marks
