     (B) Reverse repo rate is the rate at which commercial banks may park their surplus
     funds with the Central Bank.
     In order to decrease inflation in an economy, Reserve Bank of India (RBI) may
     increase the reverse repo rate. With the increase in reverse repo rate, it becomes         4
     lucrative for commercial banks to park surplus funds with the central bank.
     Consequently, this may lead to reduction in their lending capacity. Thereby, fall in the
     Aggregate Demand curbs the level of inflation.
