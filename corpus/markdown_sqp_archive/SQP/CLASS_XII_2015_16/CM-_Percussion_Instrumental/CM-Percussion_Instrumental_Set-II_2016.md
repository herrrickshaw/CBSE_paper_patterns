                                Sample Question Paper 2016

         2) Shadangas

   5. Explain any two upa pakka Vadhya Percussion instruments.

   6. Explain in detail the characteristic features of a tala Vadhya ensemble.

   7. Write short notes on any four of the following:
          a) Kaalapramanam
          b) Konnakkol
          c) Pharan
          d) Choru-Varu
          e) Chenda
