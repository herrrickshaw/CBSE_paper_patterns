           ∑ Attempt all questions.

      b) Sthayibbava and Sancharibhava

      c) Tandava and Lasya                                                                   (1.5+1.5+2)

5.    Describe the Rasa theory of Bharata. Give a detailed description of either Shringara Rasa or
      Hasya Rasa.                                                                            (2+3)

6.    Define the following:

      a) Theerumanam

      b) Thaalanga

      c) Avartana

      d) Arangetram

      e) Korvai                                                                            (1+1+1+1+1)

                                                OR

7.    Write a Korvai in two avartanas of the aditaala (chatushra jaati Triputa taala).               (5)
