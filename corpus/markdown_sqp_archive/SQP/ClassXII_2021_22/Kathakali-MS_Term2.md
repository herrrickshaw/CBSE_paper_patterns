 5   Attakadha is the technical term for Kathakali play text. The format for       3
     an Attakadha follows a fixed structure. It is compossed of slokams
     and padams. Attakadha may also include Dandakams, and less
     frequently Churnikas. Slokams are generally compossed in sanskrit
     or in manipravalam. Padams are dialogues between the characters.
     They are usually written in manipravalam. Most padams follow the
     format of the South Indian Carnatic music compositions with
     supporting pallavi, anupallavi and charanam. The drama unfolds
     through the padams.
