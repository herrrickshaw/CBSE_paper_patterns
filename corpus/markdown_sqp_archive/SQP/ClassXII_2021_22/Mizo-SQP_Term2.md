       Ahmadnagar Fort Prison Camp-a a awm laia India ram thlirna Lehkhabu a ziak
       chu a bu hmingah ‘Discovery of India’a vuah a, a dik hle a ni. India hmel hrang
        hrang, a thuk zia, ril zia, ropuizia,makzia,danglamzia hre chiang tur chuan kum
       sang tam tak dam a ngai ang. Tawngpui 18 bakah (1992) tawng tenau 1460 aia
        tam a awm a. Tawng tenau hmang ho chanchin Chhuina turin tawng khat hmang
        tan kum khat zel lo hmang ta ila, kum 1500 vel tal dam a ngai. He ram mak tak
       mai, kan chenna ram India hi zofate Pawhin tlem azawng hriat ve tum a, a
       angchhunga finna thuk tak tak inphumte hi kan tan haichhuah tuma hma kan lak
       ve a hun ta hlein ka hria..’

       ‘ Khawvel hnam fing leh vengva hmasa Grik mifinghote pawhin ‘ Music leh
       mathematics hi khawvel chahbi a ni e’ an lo ti hial a ni awm e. Glyn Evans-a
       pawhin fakna leh lawmthu sawi inhlanna rimawi hi Pathian biak dan chungnung
       ber ( highest act of worship) a lo ti hial a ni. Martin Luthera chuan music hi
       khawvela Pathian thilthlawnpek tha leh ropui ber ( finest and noblest) te zing
       ami a ni a, thalai nisi music thiam lo chu thuhriltu atan pawha nemngheh tur a
       ni lo’ a ti hial a ni. Israel hnam ropui vanglai chu music lama an thlen san ber lai,
       Davida leh Solomona ten fakna hla (Sam) an phuah chhuah hun lai kha a ni.
