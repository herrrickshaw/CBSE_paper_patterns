3.   This is a question of understanding, value-based and short answer type. A candidate is 5 marks
     expected to find out the human life values in any of the following paintings of the
     Bengal School which are included in the course of study:
             (a) Shiva and Sati (done by Nandlal Bose)
             (b) Journey’s End (done by Abanindranath Tagore)
             A candidate may perceive many and different human life values mentioned as
             following in that particular painting according to his/her own observation,
