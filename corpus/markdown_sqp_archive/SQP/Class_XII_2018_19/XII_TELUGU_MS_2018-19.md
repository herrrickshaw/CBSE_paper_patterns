        Every intelligent person is bound to have some aspirations, aims and ambitions in life. A life
without an ambition can very well be compared to a ship without a rubber which falters and flounders on
a tumultuous sea. Ambition infuses discipline and makes life meaningful. Of course, only having an
ambition is not enough. A person should strive hard to achieve his or her ambition. Without making an
effort a person cannot realize anything in life. Then his or her ambition will simply remain an idle dream.
