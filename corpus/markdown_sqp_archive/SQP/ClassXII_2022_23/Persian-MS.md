Answer 10.
We all love stories since childhood to old age. When we sit to listen to the tales
of the storyteller, we concentrate with all our six senses, we even forget to
blink. Many stories, although are sweet, but remain here and there and do not
find their way beyond the borders. While for some stories, the border between
countries is the same as the space between the words on the paper. People of all
lands, regardless of their language and script, enjoy reading and listening to
such stories.
