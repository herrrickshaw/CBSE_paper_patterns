                    Hindustani Music Percussion Instruments

                                         (Code : 036)

                                  Answer Key (2019-20)

                                            Class XII

                                                Section – I
1   a (i), b (i), C (i), d (i), e (ii) f (iv)
                                    Section II
2   The candidate is required to do the notation in proper manner of
    Notation System along with Thah Dugun & Chagun in Rupak or Tevara
    Tala.
                                        Or
    The candidate is required to do the notation of Kayada, Palta & Tihai in
    Teentala/ Aditala.
                                   Section III
3   The candidate is expected to write Laykari such as Tigun & Ada.
                                        Or
    The candidate is expected to write about any of two Gharanas of Tabla
    or Pakhawaj.
                                   Section IV
4   The candidate is to write about history of Tabla or Pakhawaj.
                                        Or
    The candidate is to write percussion instruments of Medieval period.
                                    Section V
5   The candidate is expected to write about Ustad Zakir Hussain.
                                        Or
    The candidate is expected to write about musical contribution of Pt.
    Pagal Das.
