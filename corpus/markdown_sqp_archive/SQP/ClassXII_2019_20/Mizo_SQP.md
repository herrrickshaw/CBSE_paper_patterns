      tinah Zawlbuk an lo nei vek a. He lamah hian an lo sang viau zawk a ni. Khaw hrang

      Hei hi a dik tih a lanna chu, vai len hma leh hnuah pawh pawnlam leido leh hmelma
      beihna kawngah chuan, inpun leh tan ho pawh an lo ching hman viau tawh a ni.

        A chunga thu tarlan atang hian hmanlai mizote khawsak phung thenkhat tarlang la,
