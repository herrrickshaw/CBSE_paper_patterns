       Self dependancy is verymuch needed to all, especially developing countries like
India, it is a must. We are depending on other countries a lot for technology,
employment, financial matters etc. India took thousands of crores of rupees loan

together as interest for the loan we have taken. As an effect, people has to bear the
burden of loan. If the same interest is used towards developmental works, India
would have been improved a lot by this time. Now Indian Government has started
“Make in India” programme to stop depending on other nations. Offcourse it is a
good programme if effectively brought into action. It generates employment to
youth. Brain drain can be stopped. But the thing is that people should understand the
purpose of Govt. properly and act accordingly. Then only this may be succeeded.
