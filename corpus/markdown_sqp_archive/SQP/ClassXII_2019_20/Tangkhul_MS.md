        namikhamang atamli naolila mankāhaowa kha hi 30 percent shakha mānga.

    matālengthua. Hunphun shahebli khangasan eina vāhaora chihaida hangali mi kapangluda
    hukum mihaowa. Kha chithāmāngmada hangana khipāshinaokhali tandi masāpaithuda phāla

    Hunphun kaiki mchihaowa. Ngazekzeklaga thākha kapā yangusa laga vāsa jihaowa. Kapā

    Meitei keithei zanghaowa.

   khangachee kaho marākapeiya kala shimli maungkapama naoshinao akhana. Ava-avā

   maleishimana. Phāshak phākazā thārānla hanmāi phālāka kala shāishi zāshiakha
   kharartheirarei haiya. Chieina kasāng khara shimkhurli khikha mangachonla yānglā zatta
   khai, sā phāshaizatta.
   Hithāda awui khangarik khangaror kala sāshāt china thangkha mathangkha kashong meirong

   maleilāk mana. Awui malung kaping chieina vāshumda rāi kapinga mi akha thuisāpapam
   haowa.
