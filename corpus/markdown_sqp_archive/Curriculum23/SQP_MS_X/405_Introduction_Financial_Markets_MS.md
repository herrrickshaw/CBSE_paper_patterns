          If the overall stock or bond

          economic factors, the value of

          fund’s portfolio can drop, thereby
          impacting the fund performance.
          (1m)

          Bad news about an individual
          company can pull down its stock
          price, which can negatively affect
          fund holdings. This risk can be
          reduced by having a diversified

          variety of stocks drawn from
          different industries.(1m)

          Bond prices and interest rates

          prices fall and this decline in
          underlying securities affects the
          fund negatively. (1m)

          when the funds invest in
          corporate bonds, they run the risk

          fall in the value of the bond
          causing the NAV of the fund to
          take a
          beating. (1m)
