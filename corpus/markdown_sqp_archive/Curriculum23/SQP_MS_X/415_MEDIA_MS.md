Q. 20 Answer – The uses of Specular Maps are:          CBSE Study Material   3   98   4
      1. Some surfaces are shinier than others. By
      mapping a texture to the specular attribute
      of an object’s material, we create a specular
      map which lets us to describe how shine
      appear on objects.
      2. Specular highlights show the places on the
      object where the light sources are reflected
      at consistent angles; reflections on an object
      show, among other things, light bounced
      from surrounding objects.
      3. Specular highlights depend directly on the
      view (camera), not on the position of the
      light, like diffuse shading does.
      4. The size of a specular highlight on a
      surface makes the surface look either flat or
      shiny.
