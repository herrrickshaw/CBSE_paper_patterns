        percentage of the total ordered
        observations at or below it. Therefore, pth
        percentile of a distribution is the value such
        that p percentage of the ordered
        observation falls at or below it.
        Quartiles of dataset partitions the data into
        four equal parts, with one-fourth of the data
        values in each part. The total of 100% is
        divided into four equal parts: 25%, 50%, 75%
        & 100%. Since the median is defined as the
        middlemost value in the observation, the
        median will have 50% of the observations at
        or below it.

        quartiles sort the data into four quarters,
        deciles sort the data into ten equal parts:
        the 10th, 20th, 30th, 40th, 50th, 60th, 70th,
