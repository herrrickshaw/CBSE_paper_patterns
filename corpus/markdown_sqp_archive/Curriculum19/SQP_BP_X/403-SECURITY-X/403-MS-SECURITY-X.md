Question 16                                                              (1/2 Mark each)

Answer: Rules/ guidelines for private security staff regarding arresting/detaining are:

   1. Private security personnel, like any other citizen can assist police or magistrate in the
      arrest of any person wanted by law.
   2. Security personnel can detain a person who is a proclaimed offender or has
      committed any non-bailable and cognizable offence.
   3. Male security guards will not arrest or detain a female
   4. Police will be informed about such detention and hand over the person to police as
      early as possible
