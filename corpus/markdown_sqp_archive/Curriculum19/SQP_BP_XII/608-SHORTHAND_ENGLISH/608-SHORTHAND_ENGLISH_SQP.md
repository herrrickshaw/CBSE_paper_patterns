                                  SHORTHAND (ENGLISH) (608)

                                               (2018-19)

                                              CLASS XII

                                              (THEORY)

Time Allowed: 2 Hours                                                  Maximum Marks: 30

                                             N O T E
   i)         Answers should be written in English only, otherwise the answers will be cancelled.
   ii)        Answers should be specific, to the point, with examples and Shorthand outlines.
   iii)       The Question Paper contains 21 Questions out of which 16 Questions are to be

              attempted.
   iv)



                                                  SECTION - A


   Attempt any 10 Questions out of the following. Each question carries one mark:
                                                                 (10X1) =10


          Fill in the Blanks with suitable words in the following questions:

  1. Diphone signs are placed on ___________ places of a Consonant.

  2. Prefix ‘Self’ is represented by a_____________ circle to be written close
     to the following stroke in the second vowel place.

  3. Contractions are generally written __________ the line.

  4. Doubling Principle is used in forming Advanced Phrases for the words_______.

  5. Stroke ‘S’ is employed to represent the word _________ in forming Intersections.

          Answer the following questions in not more than 25 words each:

  6. Explain by giving suitable examples the rules for writing Consonant ‘L’
      in downward direction.

  7. How is the Prefix ‘Magna/Magni/Magne’ represented in Shorthand?
