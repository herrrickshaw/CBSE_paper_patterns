                                 Case Study based questions
Read the passage carefully and answer the Questions no. 14 to 18.

     When we wear clothes, they become dirty and need laundering. The dirt
     is due to stains, dust, grease and perspiration. Stains and dirt left on
     fabrics become a medium for the growth of bacteria, fungus or other
     harmful organisms, which may lead to skin problems and poor personal
     hygiene. The dirt and stains also reduce the strength of the fabric and its
     freshness.

     However, clothes need not be washed every time they are worn. It
     depends on the weather in which these have been worn and how much
     sweat they have absorbed. When the weather is good, one can safely
     reuse them. But before putting them away for reuse one should do
     shaking, brushing, and airing to remove loose dirt and bad odours from
     clothes.
