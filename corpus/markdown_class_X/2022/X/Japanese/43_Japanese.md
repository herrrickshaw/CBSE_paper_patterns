                      INSTRUCTIONS FOR CANDIDATES
The examination is of 40 marks in total. There are four sections namely; Reading,
Writing, Grammar and Kanji. Each section has 10 marks each. Every section has
options for the candidates to choose according to their own choice.

answer only 10 questions.

8 questions.
In Section C, there are 14 questions and candidates are required to answer only
10 questions.
