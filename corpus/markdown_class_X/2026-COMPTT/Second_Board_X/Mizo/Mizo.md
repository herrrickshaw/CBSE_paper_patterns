5. A hnuaia thu hi uluk takin chhiar la, a tawpa zawhna awmte hi chhang
ang che.
Nunah hian hlim reng hi thil harsa tak a ni. A chang chuan kan nui a, a
chang chuan kan tap thin. Hrisel lai a awm a, hrisel loh lai pawh a awm
thei. Nun hi a pangngaiin a kal reng thei lo a, mihring pawhin rilru ngai
reng kan pu thei hek lo. Kan chawhmeh duh zawngte a danglam fo a, tui
kan tih thlengin a danglam fo thin. Kum khat chhungin zan a ni reng thei
lo a, chhun pawh a awm ve bawk. Khawvelah hian thil a inthlak kual reng
a chhunga cheng mihringte pawh an inthlak nasa em em bawk.
Danglam ve ngai lo pakhat a awm a, chu chu 'Hun' hi a ni. Hun hian
engkim a thlak a, danglamna thlentu, danglam ve ngai si lo a ni. Thil thar
kan beisei a, thil ngai bawk a lo thleng leh thin. A ngai renga awm theih te
kan chak a, eng emaw deuh a danglam ve te pawh kan chak bawk. Mahse,
danglamna a thleng a, danglam thin mihringte leh thil siamte hi hun
