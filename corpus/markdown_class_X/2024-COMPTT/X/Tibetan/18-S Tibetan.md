```
                               The Rabbit and the Tortoises
       Once upon a time there was a rabbit and tortoise. They were good friends. They
       used to meet and play every day. The rabbit always boasted that he could run
       faster than the tortoise.
       So they decided to have a race. They chose a starting and finishing point. The
       rabbit ran really fast and soon left the tortoise far behind. He thought that the
       tortoise is too slow and he can rest for a while. So he stopped under a tree and
       went to sleep. Mean while tortoise kept walking the whole time and reached the
       winning point. When the rabbit woke up he saw that tortoise has already won
       the race.
```
