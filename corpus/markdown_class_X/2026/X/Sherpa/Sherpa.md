                   Tradition is when people do the same things that their family
          or community has done for a long time. It helps people remember
          their past and feel close to their culture. Families may celebrate
          special holidays, cook favorite foods, or wear certain clothes.
          Traditions also include songs, dances, stories, and special events.
          They teach people to respect others and work together. Keeping
          traditions is important because it connects us to our roots and each
          other.
