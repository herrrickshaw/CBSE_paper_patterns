5.   A hnuaia thu te hi uluk takin chhiar la a tawpa zawhna te hi chhang ang
     che.
            Thihna hi mihringte la tawh vek tur a ni. Dam rei awm mah se dam
     hlen an awm ngai lo. Kan hma hunah tunge kan nih ang a, eng eizawnna
     nge kan la neih ang, mi lar kan la ni ang em. Mi hausa kan ni ang em tih
     kan chian theih loh lai pawha kan nuna thil lo thleng tur chiang tak chu’
     kan la thi dawn’ tih hi a ni. Eng chen nge kan la dam dawn erawh Tu
     mahin kan hre thei lo. Vawiina mi nung, naktuka ruang ni maithei vek
     kan nih avang hian tu mah hi inhmusil leh inbe chhe tur kan awm lo.
     Thihna hi tawp hlenna a ni lo a, thih hnu piahah khawvel dang la awmin
     kan ring vek bawk. Kan thih hun tur kan lungkham luat avanga rilru hah
     a sawt lo a, kan thih hun piah suahtuah vak lah hi a sawt hek lo. Vawiin
     hman that mai hian kan nunah malsawmna a thlen a, mahni inhmangaih
     hi mi dang kan hmangaih theih nana kawng zawh tur hmasa a ni. Kan
     dam ang a, kan la thi vek dawn a, kan thih hmaa midangte tana thil tha
     kan tih hi thil hlu leh pawimawh em em a ni a, kan hlutna erawh kan thil
     tha tihah a innghat thung.
     Thihna hi mihringte thil tawrh zinga na ber leh rapthlak ber a ni hial ang.
     A thi-a te tan chuan engmah an hriat tawh miau loh avangin thawlehna
     nghakin an muhil chu a ni mai. Mahse hetah hian a tawp hauh lo an
     chhungte, thiante leh anmahni hmangaihtuteah ve thung chuan nghawng
     nasa tak a neiin khawharna te, nguina te leh lunglenna, tahna a thlen
     nasa em em a ni.
     Thil tihbaiawm tak chu thihna hi a ni ve reng a, Lal Isua krista pawh
     khan a thih dawn meuh kha chuan a hreh ve hle a nih kha. A tlangpuiin
     mihringte hian thihna hrim hrim hi a thim zawngin kan thlir thin a,
     Amaherawhchu kawng danga kan thlir chuan thihna hi mihring nun
     tihreawmtu zawng zawngte kianna leh nun khawih buai theitu zawng
     zawngte ralna a ni ve thei bawk. Damlo natna khuma mu na tuar hreawm
     ti tak ten dam thlakhlelh loh chang an nei fo. An tawrh a nat em avangin
     thih mai chu hahdamthlak zawkin an hre thin. Chu tih rualin dam la
     thlahlel ngawih ngawih sum leh pai tam tak senga an dam theihna tura
     theih tawp chhuah mek an awm ve leh zel bawk a. Kan zavaia kan hriat
     tur chu mihringte hi thihna hma a kun za theuh tur kan ni a, khawvel hi
     kan khualzinna ram mai a ni, kan awm reng theih dawn loh avangin. He
     khawvela kan awm chhung hian mite tan malsawmna nih tum tlat hi kan
     thil tha tih tur pawimawh tak a ni a, khawvela thil awm zawng zawngte
     hi ral vek tur an nih avangin thlakhlelh tur reng a awm lo va. Kan
     damchhung hunte hi thawnthu ang lek an ni tih hi i vawng reng ang u.
