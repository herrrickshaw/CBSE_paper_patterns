      (a)   The National Flag of India is made up of three colours. They are
            Saffron, White and Green. The Flag is, therefore, called the ‘‘Tiranga’’
            or the Tricolour. Saffron stands for courage. White stands for peace.
            Green stands for progress and plenty. In the centre of the flag is the
            Ashoka Chakra. This stands for love and peace. The Flag is hoisted on
            national days such as Republic Day and Independence Day.
                                        OR
      (b)   One day, a cap seller sat under a tree to rest. After some time, he
            went to sleep. As he was sleeping, some monkeys from the tree came
            down and took away all of his caps. When the cap seller awoke, he
            saw all his caps were missing. He looked up the tree and saw the
            monkeys were wearing the caps. The cap seller thought of an idea to
            get his caps back. He threw down his cap in anger. The monkeys also
            imitated him. They threw all the caps on the ground. The cap seller
            picked up all the caps.
