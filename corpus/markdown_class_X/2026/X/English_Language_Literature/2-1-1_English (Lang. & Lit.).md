savoury while in Japan, fluffy "soufflé pancakes" have become a
modern favourite.
(3)
The American version of pancakes, thick and soft, gained
popularity in the 18th century. The use of baking powder made

pancakes became a universal dish, symbolizing community and
celebration. Whether sweet or savoury, thick or thin, every
culture has found a way to adapt this simple food to its own
taste and traditions.
